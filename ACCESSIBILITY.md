# Accessibility Statement — psivicom.github.io

## 🎯 Our Commitment
The WENDY architecture and the PSIVI.COM research community are committed to ensuring that our open-source software, FAIR data, and digital apiary tools are accessible to all researchers, developers, and citizen scientists, including those with disabilities. 

As the lead maintainer operates with a disability that may affect typing accuracy and workflow pacing, our architecture inherently embraces asynchronous, accessible, and resilient design patterns. We rely on strict CI/CD automation, lock-free concurrent structures, and robust voice/code interfaces to bypass physical limitations.

## 🛰️ Supported Environments
Our web interfaces (e.g., `openscience.html`, `wendy-console.html`) and data exports aim to comply with **WCAG 2.1 AA** standards. 
- **Telemetry Exports:** Our FAIR data is strictly exported in machine-readable, screen-reader-friendly formats like **JSON-LD** and **HDF5**, ensuring zero information loss for assistive technologies.
- **APIs & Scripts:** CLI tools are designed with clear, descriptive error outputs (`K_neg` immune responses) to aid developers using screen readers.

## 🐝 Known Limitations & Ongoing Compost Protocol
- Some raw data visualizations from space-research and apiary gradient sensors may require alternative text descriptions, which are actively generated via our automated `Compost_Protocol` for failed visual states.
- We continuously refine our GitHub Actions to ensure CI/CD outputs are cleanly formatted for accessibility tools.

## 📞 Reporting Barriers
If you encounter an accessibility barrier, a broken stigmergy gradient, or a telemetry export that is not fully perceivable:
1. Open an **Issue** on this repository using the `🐛 Bug Report` template.
2. Contact the primary researcher at [psivi.com](https://psivi.com).

*We view accessibility as a fundamental component of Open Science. No node is left behind.*
