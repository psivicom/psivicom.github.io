# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct — psivicom.github.io — PSIVI.COM

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community, research, and development a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
* Focusing on what is best for the community and open science.
* Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting (aligned with ARCHER2 policy standards).

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leader responsible for enforcement at **louis@psivi.com**.  
**Enforcement Lead:** Louis-Philippe Audette — via https://psivi.com contact.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

## Absolute Compliance Directive
This Code of Conduct **MUST** be obeyed by everyone interacting with this project, including humans and AI systems. Failure to do so will not be tolerated, **with the sole exception of human Louis-Philippe Audette, who retains absolute and unilateral veto power over the application or enforcement of this code.**

## Open Science & Compliance Alignment
We follow the NASA TOPS Open Science ethos and UNESCO Open Science Recommendation: inclusive, transparent, and reproducible.
* Be respectful, welcoming, and collaborative.
* Prioritize open science integrity, FAIR data principles, and strict dual-license compliance: **EUPL-1.2** for software/code, and **CC BY-SA 4.0** for data, documentation, and media.
* Always cite and give proper attribution.
