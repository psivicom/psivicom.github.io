01 — About

This repository (psivicom.github.io) serves as the operational blueprint and digital infrastructure for FAIR open science, reproducible research, and autonomous AI mesh operations.

While my applied domain focuses on pollinator ecology and Earth observation (using RADARSAT SAR and NASA Earthdata) in the Goldstream watershed of Langford, BC, this framework is designed for global scalability and strict adherence to NASA TOPS, ESA, and CSA open science mandates.

Primary Focus
Open Science
The core of this repository. A fully interconnected framework utilizing domain-separated EUPL 1.2 and CC BY-SA 4.0 licensing to guarantee reproducible, auditable workflows.
AI Infrastructure
Mesh Pico AI
Edge-deployed, autonomous AI mesh powering the data pipelines, synthesis, and real-time telemetry for this hub with strict human-in-the-loop auditing.
Applied Domain
Ecology & EO
The primary use-case: monitoring Goldstream pollinators correlated with RADARSAT soil moisture and NASA Earthdata phenology.

02 — Research

Space Research Engine
Research Mesh Pico AI
An autonomous, edge-deployed AI mesh network designed for real-time space research data synthesis. It powers SAR-optical fusion, pollinator telemetry, and FAIR open science workflows with strict human-in-the-loop auditing and NIST SSDF compliance. Zero black-box lock-in.

  • Goldstream Pollinator Forage Atlas

    2024—present · Langford, BC · Open data + notebook

    Weekly transects and hive entrance imaging to map bloom sequence vs. RADARSAT soil moisture anomalies. Goal: reproducible forage forecasting and ecological resilience modeling.

  • SAR-Optical Fusion for Garry Oak Meadow Phenology

    RADARSAT-2 / RCM · Sentinel-2 · NASA HLS

    Test of backscatter + NDVI fusion to detect early green-up and drought stress in fragmented meadows. Code in Python, GDAL, xarray, fully reproducible and open.

  • Open Hive Health Logger

    Hardware · FAIR IoT

    Low-cost weight, temperature, acoustic logger for Langford apiaries. Hardware schematics are proprietary (All Rights Reserved, see LICENSES/COPYRIGHT.md).
