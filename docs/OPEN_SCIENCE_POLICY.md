## 1. Mission Statement

PSIVI.COM is dedicated to advancing open, transparent, and reproducible scientific research. We believe that scientific knowledge, software tools, and research data should be freely accessible, interoperable, and reusable by the global community, in strict alignment with the **FAIR Principles** (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) and the open-science mandates of NASA, ESA, and CSA.

## 2. Licensing & Intellectual Property

We employ a strict, domain-separated licensing framework to maximize open-science impact while protecting specific commercial and physical intellectual property:

- **Open Source Software:** All code, scripts, configurations, and workflows are licensed under the **European Union Public Licence v. 1.2 (EUPL 1.2)**. This ensures strong copyleft protection against closed-source hijacking while maintaining seamless legal compatibility with global space agencies.
- **Open Data & Documentation:** All research data, datasets, documentation, OSDMPs, and media are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**. This mandates proper attribution (The Audette Clause) and ensures that derivative research remains openly accessible to the community.
- **Hardware & Physical IP:** All hardware designs, schematics, PCB layouts, and physical implementations are **All Rights Reserved**. These are managed completely separately under the commercial entity at [w-1-n.com](https://w-1-n.com). This repository and website contain architectural theory, data, and software only.

## 3. Reproducibility & Archival

- **Version Control:** All research code and documentation are strictly version-controlled via Git.
- **Persistent Identifiers:** Major research releases, datasets, and software versions are archived via **Zenodo** and/or the **Open Science Framework (OSF)** to mint permanent, citable DOIs.
- **Citation:** Users of this research are required to cite the work using the provided `CITATION.cff` or Zenodo DOI. See [LICENSE_COMPLIANCE.md](LICENSE_COMPLIANCE.md) for mandatory attribution requirements.

## 4. AI-Assisted Research Transparency

In accordance with NASA TOPS and institutional guidance on AI use in open science:
- AI-assisted workflows (e.g., code generation, data structuring, formatting) are utilized to accelerate development and ensure high-assurance compliance.
- **No AI-generated content is published without rigorous human review, validation, and accountability.**
- The Principal Investigator (Louis-Philippe Audette) retains full responsibility for the scientific accuracy, licensing, and integrity of all published work.

## 5. Timestamps & Provenance

To ensure absolute reproducibility and alignment with NASA SPD-41a standards, all data, logs, and file updates utilize strict Zulu time (UTC) with millisecond precision (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.sssZ`).
